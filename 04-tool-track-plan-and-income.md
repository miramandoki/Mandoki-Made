### Legal and compliance risks (flag, do not decide)
- **Personal data**: the CRM stores buyer data. You become a processor. You need a data processing agreement and a clear privacy setup. The agent-contact scraper is a separate and bigger risk (see file 03).
- **AI output**: the sales agent must not state prices, availability or legal status that the client has not supplied. Add human review and a disclaimer.
- **Liability**: limit it in the contract. Have a lawyer draft terms.
- **Cross-border invoicing and VAT**: selling from Portugal or Sweden to clients in other countries has VAT and invoicing rules. Ask your accountant which entity sells, and keep it separate from your employer and other businesses.
- **Third-party licences**: check the terms of every API, model and data source in the tool.

## Course vs tool vs both

| | Course | Tool packages | Both (course first) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time to first revenue | Days (pre-sale), weeks (full course) | 6 to 8 weeks | Days, then tool at week 8 |
| Upfront effort | Medium: video and worksheets | High: packaging and legal | Medium, then high |
| Revenue per customer | €300 to €500 | €1,500 to €9,000 | Both |
| Scales without you | Yes | No, delivery and support are manual | Partly |
| Legal risk | Low to medium | Medium to high | Medium |
| Risk of being wrong | Testable in 10 days | Needs customers to test | Course test reduces tool risk |
| Audience building | Yes | No | **Yes** |

**Recommendation: both, in this order.** Course first, tool second. The course validates demand cheaply in days, builds an audience and shows which agents have budget. Tool packages then go to the best 10 to 20 course leads as a paid next step. If the course test fails, you have lost about €700 and 10 days, not two months of packaging.
