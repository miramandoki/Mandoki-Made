Goal: learn in about 10 days whether enough of the right agents want this and will pay, before recording the course. Budget cap: €250 to €750 (your answer), proposed €500 with a hard stop.

Subhead: A short, practical course for agents who work with international buyers on branded residences and off-plan developments. Early access list now open.

International buyers cannot walk through what does not exist. They need trust, clear visuals and calm guidance. Most agents learn this by trial and error. This course shows a tested way to do it, from someone who has sold luxury off-plan and builds the visuals herself.

- A clear sales process from first enquiry to contract

**Who it is for**
Agents, brokers and developer sales teams who sell, or want to sell, unbuilt luxury property to buyers abroad.

## Questionnaire (branded form plus one-page doc)

Use your ten questions as written. Additions I recommend:

- Intro line: "Three minutes. Your answers shape the course. No sales call."
- Question 1 as a dropdown by country or region, plus free text.
- Question 2 as ranges: 0, 1 to 5, 6 to 15, 16 to 40, 40+.
- Question 7 and 8: add "I would pay today for early access" as a yes or no, and price ranges in bands: under €150, €150 to €300, €300 to €500, €500 to €800, over €800.
- Add question 11 (optional): "May we follow up by email about the course?" This is a separate, unticked consent box.
- Consent text: "I agree to be contacted by email about this course. I can unsubscribe at any time. Privacy notice: [link]."

The questionnaire collects personal data. Controller name, privacy notice and retention period must be set before the form goes live. Ask your lawyer or a data-protection adviser to review the notice.

One-page doc layout: title, three lines on who you are, the ten questions, one line on how answers are used, contact. Build it as a PDF for LinkedIn posts and partner emails.

## Paid ad test plan

**Hypotheses to test**
1. Agents click and sign up when the message is "sell unbuilt luxury to international buyers".
2. The branded residence angle converts at least as well as the broader off-plan angle.
3. Some will put down a small refundable reservation.

**Setup**
- Creative: the 45 to 90 second intro video, cut to 30 and 15 seconds. Two thumbnails.
- Two ad sets, same budget: A "Branded residences" angle, B "Off-plan and AI visuals" angle.
- Channel: LinkedIn first (agent job titles, countries of interest). Optional second channel: Meta, narrow interests, only if the budget allows.
- Duration: 7 to 10 days.

**Budget**
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Ad spend, hard cap | €500 |
| Reserve for a second creative if one ad set clearly wins | €150 |
| Tools (form, email, page) | €0 to €50 on free tiers |
| Total cap | €700 |

Cost reality check: sources show LinkedIn cost per click for Europe around $5 to $6, and cost per lead for Europe around $120 to $150 in general B2B ([Factors.ai](https://www.factors.ai/blog/linkedin-ads-cost), [SearchLab](https://searchlab.nl/kosten/wat-kost-linkedin-adverteren), [The Ad Spend](https://theadspend.com/blog/linkedin-ads-emea)). No real estate specific benchmark exists. At €500 and a €5 click cost, expect about 100 clicks, so the paid test gives a small sample. **Treat it as a signal, not proof.** Combine it with organic LinkedIn posts and direct invitations to people who know you.

**Metrics to track**
- Click-through rate on each ad set
- Landing page conversion to email sign-up
- Questionnaire completion rate
- Share who answer "I would pay today" and choose a price band at or above €297
- Reservations or booked 15-minute calls
- Cost per sign-up and cost per qualified sign-up

**Go or no-go thresholds (my proposals, adjust them)**

| Result after 10 days | Decision |
|---|---|
| 40 or more qualified sign-ups (agents who sell international or unbuilt) AND 10 or more say they would pay at €297 or above AND 5 or more paid reservations or booked calls | **Go.** Record the course. |
| 20 to 39 qualified sign-ups OR strong answers but few reservations | **Iterate.** Change the angle or audience, run one more week, cap stays. |
| Under 20 qualified sign-ups AND under 3 reservations | **No-go** for this topic. Test the runner-up or switch to the tool track. |

Write down the thresholds before launch and do not move them afterwards.

## Compliant outreach (no mass emailing of scraped lists)

**Do not** email the scraped agent list. Cold email to individuals in Portugal needs prior consent under the e-privacy rules (Lei 41/2004, article 13-A according to a Portuguese data-protection source). B2B rules are less clear, and the sources I found are vendor blogs. Scraped personal data also carries GDPR duties, such as transparency and a lawful basis, that are hard to meet at scale. **Ask a Portuguese or Swedish data-protection lawyer to review the scraper and its data before any use.** I did not verify the statute text.

**Compliant routes**
1. **Opt-in first**: landing page, questionnaire, lead magnet (for example a one-page "Unbuilt buyer fear checklist"), double opt-in email.
2. **LinkedIn**: weekly posts, short carousels, 3D before and after reveals, and personal connection notes (not bulk messaging). Respect LinkedIn terms.
3. **Partnerships**: international agencies, developer sales teams, proptech and visualisation vendors, relocation and mortgage broker communities. Offer a free guest session or a co-branded checklist.
4. **Communities**: answer questions in agent groups and forums. Do not post links without value.
5. **Content marketing**: short videos from the course, one useful article per week, a YouTube channel.
6. **Warm network**: personal invitations to people who already know you, with a clear way to say no.

Each route needs a visible unsubscribe or opt-out and a stored record of consent.
