## Why go
- **Primary data.** The report is the most relevant Portuguese data source for the course topic. It may settle the unverified branded premium figure and Portugal's European rank (see files 01 and 06).
- **Audience test.** The room is the target audience: developers, agents and brand people working on Portuguese branded residences.
- **Partners.** Four organisations are potential partners or guest voices: a tech or data firm, a bathroom and design brand, an AI company, and the report publisher.
- **No cost.** It costs a morning, which suits the validation budget.

## Before you go
1. Request the invitation now. Capacity is limited and the event is 8 days away.
2. Decide in which capacity you attend: your employer's role, or as the course builder. Do not mix the two. If you attend for your employer, check whether talking about a side venture is appropriate. This is for you to decide, with a lawyer if in doubt.
3. Ask the organiser if the report will be shared and in which language.
4. Print 20 one-page questionnaires with a QR code to the opt-in form (file 03). Never collect contacts without a clear consent line.
5. Prepare a one-sentence description of your project that names no past projects, developers or employers.

- "What do your buyers ask for that nobody teaches your sales team?"
- "How do you show the product before it is built?"
- "Which markets do your international buyers come from now, and has that changed?"
- "If a short online course for agents existed on selling unbuilt branded product, what would make it worth paying for?"
