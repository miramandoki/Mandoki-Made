# 01. Ranked topic table and verdict

Status: first research pass, 3 Nov 2026. The search tool I used is US-only and returned thin results for course marketplaces. Competitor counts and review data for Udemy, Teachable, Thinkific, Maven and Skillshare are therefore **not verified** and must be checked by hand (see "Manual checks" at the end). Scores are my judgement from the evidence below, not measured data.

## Verdict

**Golden topic (working title): selling unbuilt luxury property to international buyers.**
Flagship case: branded residences and lifestyle-brand developments. Differentiator: 3D visuals and AI used to sell before completion.

Why this beats the other candidates:

- Demand is real and growing in your home market. Savills counts 33 branded residence projects in Portugal, 18 of them in the pipeline. Sources differ on whether that places Portugal 3rd or higher in Europe.
- I found no dedicated online course for agents on branded residences or on AI-assisted off-plan selling. The closest items are an in-person Portuguese luxury course at €1,750, a €499 luxury agent certification, a $545 Canadian self-paced course and general luxury certifications from NAR. None of them is specific to unbuilt product.
- It fits your experience better than any other option: off-plan luxury sales plus a 3D studio running since 2014. Nobody else can credibly teach both halves.
- It needs no Portuguese and no legal advice from you. Legal and tax parts can stay as "know the issue, call your lawyer".

Main risk: the audience for pure branded residences is small. Fix: position the course for anyone selling unbuilt luxury, with branded residences as the showcase case. The validation test should compare the two framings (see file 03).

## Evidence by topic

### Branded residences
- Savills Branded Residences Europe 2026 (via press): 33 projects in Portugal, of which 18 are in the pipeline. Source: [Abode2](https://www.abode2.com/branded-residences-propel-portugal-market-into-europes-top-3/), [Essential Business](https://www.essential-business.pt/2026/04/01/portugal-considered-one-of-the-most-dynamic-countries-in-branded-residences/), [idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/news/node/74752).
- Premium: **the 38% figure in your brief could not be verified.** Sources show about 33% globally and 29% in Europe in an earlier Savills overview, and 20 to 25% for Lisbon and the Algarve ([Savills Portugal blog](https://en.savills.pt/blog/article/373955-0/international-property/branded-residences-in-portugal---a-growing-opportunity-for-developers-and-investors-.aspx)). Do not use 38% in public copy until you have read the report itself.
- A second search attributes 38% to **Dubai** in a Savills comparison (Miami 32%, London 8%). Your 38% may be that Dubai figure. See [06-branded-residence-examples.md](06-branded-residence-examples.md).
- Algarve is described as Europe's fastest-growing branded segment, with Comporta and Algarve operators named ([Quinta Property summary](https://quintaproperty.com/research/savills-branded-residences-2025-2026/)). This is a local agency summary.
- Courses: no dedicated branded residence course found in this pass.

### AI and 3D visualisation for off-plan
- Off-plan buyers are about a third of buyers in Australia according to REA, which took a stake in a 3D off-plan platform ([MI3](https://mi-3.com.au/03-10-2024/rea-group-dives-3d-visualisation-minority-stake-immersiv)). Shows the category attracts investment.
- Agent AI use: NAR 2026 Technology Report says 23% of agents use AI daily and 25% weekly ([Inman](https://www.inman.com/2026/09/22/nar-technology-report-ai-adoption/)). US data, but it shows adoption outpacing skills.
- Training: only vendor pages and service agencies surfaced. No course.

### Non-resident financing
- Content is abundant and free: [idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/en/news/financial-advice-in-portugal/2025/07/18/5866-how-to-get-a-mortgage-in-portugal-as-a-foreigner), [Portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/mortgage-portugal/), [Property Guides](https://www.propertyguides.com/portugal/buying/mortgages/).
- Facts conflict: loan-to-value is quoted from 60% to 80%, deposits 30 to 40%. Most sources are brokers with commercial interests. This topic needs verified bank data before teaching it, and it is a regulated area.
- Best use: one lesson inside the golden course, not a course of its own.

### International demand
- idealista data (2026): UK and US lead international demand in 12 of 20 major cities. Lisbon non-resident share of online demand about 10%, Porto 12% ([idealista, Jul 2026](https://www.idealista.pt/en/news/property-for-sale-in-portugal/2026/07/31/76816-where-foreign-property-buyers-are-choosing-in-portugal-in-2026)).
- Luxury: foreigners were 27% of luxury demand in Q1 2025, with the UK at 15% and the US at 13.1% of international interest ([idealista, Apr 2025](https://www.idealista.pt/en/news/property-for-sale-in-portugal/2025/04/22/69213-britons-and-americans-among-top-foreign-buyers-of-luxury-homes-in-portugal)).
- Headwind: policy changes have reduced tax advantages for foreign buyers (same source). Teach the course as "sell better in a tougher market".
