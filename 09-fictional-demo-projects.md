# 09. Fictional demo projects

Why: there is no written contract with the first client, and they would not want their project used as a case study. So every demo, course slide and ad uses **fictional projects built from scratch**. Nothing is reused from the client: no floor plans, renders, brand assets, copy, prices or data.

Every demo shows the line: "Fictional project created for demonstration. Not for sale."

Name check: I have not checked that these names are free. Check before use so they do not copy a real project or brand.

## Project A: Casa Lumen, Lisbon
| Item | Detail (all fictional) |
|---|---|
| Concept | Rehabilitation of a 1920s residential building on a quiet street in a central Lisbon district |
| Units | 14: four T1, six T2, three T3, one T3 duplex penthouse with a 60 m² terrace |
| Areas | 55 to 175 m² |
| Shared | Courtyard garden, bicycle room, concierge app |
| Completion | Q4 2028 |
| Story | Light, original tiles, calm city living |
| Shows | Urban rehab sales, 3D plans, furnished layouts, the AI assistant on unit questions |

## Project B: Ondine Residences, Silver Coast
| Item | Detail (all fictional) |

Location choice: neither project uses the client's area, and neither uses the coastal areas of your other past work.

## Sample data (in `demo-data/`)
- `units.csv`: 38 fictional units with typology, m², outdoor space, parking, price, €/m², status and completion date. Prices are invented for the demo and are **not market data**.
- `leads.csv`: 30 fictional buyer leads with nationality, budget, financing, source, pipeline stage and next action. Emails use the reserved example.com domain.
