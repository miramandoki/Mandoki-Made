# 06. Branded residence examples worth teaching

"Best" is subjective. This list is chosen for **teaching value** (clear story, public data, relevant to your buyers), not ranked by sales volume. Facts come from press coverage found in one search pass. I did not read the primary developer or brand sources, so check each before using it in public copy.

Rule for public material: do not link any example here to your own past work, clients or employers.

## Premium facts, handle with care

- Savills figures reported in the search: global average premium about 31% in one analysis, with Dubai at 38%, Miami at 32% and London at 8% in a later one. Knight Frank reports about 30% in general and 86% in Dubai.
- **The 38% in your brief may be the Dubai figure, not a European one.** Please confirm in the Savills report itself before it appears anywhere public.
- Earlier results show 29% for Europe and 20 to 25% for Lisbon and the Algarve.

## How to use this in the course
- Module 2 uses 4 or 5 of these as short, sourced case slides: one hotel brand, one fashion brand, one designer-led, one Portuguese.
- Module 8 stays on your own anonymised case, not on these.
- Lesson on claims: show how to quote premiums with city and date, and how to avoid a single global number.
