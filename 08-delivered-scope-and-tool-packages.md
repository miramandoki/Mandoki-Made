## Flags before selling or showing this to anyone else
- **Client permission.** Showing a client's project name, brand, floor plans or sales data in a public demo, a course or an ad needs the client's written consent. Check your contract for ownership and reuse terms. A lawyer should review.
- **Rename the demos.** At least two demo addresses carry the client's name. Use neutral demo names, sample data and neutral branding before sharing with prospects.
- **Access keys.** A demo access key was included in the message. Rotate it if it has been shared widely. I have not copied it anywhere.
- **Personal data in the CRM.** Selling Amigo makes the seller a processor of buyers' data. A data processing agreement and privacy notice are needed. Use only synthetic sample data in demos.
- **AI assistant.** It must only state facts the client approved. No prices, availability or legal status unless supplied. Add a disclaimer and human review.
- **Entity and conflicts.** Confirm which company delivered the work (Mandoki Made, Artificial Monks, or another), who invoiced, and whether any conflict of interest or confidentiality duty arises from your other roles. I do not know the arrangement and have not assumed one.
- **Public wording.** In all public material, keep to "a Lisbon residential rehabilitation project". Do not name the client, developers or employers.
