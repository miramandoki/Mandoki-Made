# 02. Course design, intro script and platform

Working title: **Sell the Unbuilt**. Subtitle: how to sell branded residences and off-plan luxury to international buyers. Placeholder only, see brand names in file 03.

## Format
- English only, self-paced video, international audience of agents, brokers and developer sales teams.
- No mandatory live sessions. Optional quarterly live Q&A and a light community.
- Hybrid presenter: Mira on camera in the intro and four key lessons. The rest is animated 3D walkthroughs, screen recordings and anonymised case studies.
- Lessons of 10 to 15 minutes. Each ends with one worksheet or template and one action for the week.
- Total length target for version 1: 8 modules, about 3 to 4 hours of video. Smaller is better for launch.

## Module outline

| # | Module | Lessons (10 to 15 min each) | Take-away asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why unbuilt is different | What buyers fear when nothing exists yet. The trust gap at a distance. What a branded residence adds. | Buyer fear map |
| 2 | Branded residences, plainly | How the brand, developer and operator relate. What the premium really is and what it is not. What to say and not say about returns. | One-page explainer you can send to buyers |
| 3 | Positioning and storytelling with visuals | Writing the story of a place before it exists. Choosing which visuals to commission. Lifestyle over specification. | Positioning worksheet |
| 4 | Visuals that sell before completion | Stills, animation, interactive walkthroughs. What AI can and cannot do. Honest disclosure of what is a render. | Visual brief template and checklist |
| 5 | Financing for international buyers | Deposit, valuation gap, closing costs, timelines, currency risk. Where a mortgage broker must take over. | Financing questions checklist (flag: not financial advice) |
| 6 | Sales process, first enquiry to contract | Qualifying, first call, presentation, reservation, promissory contract stage, follow-up cadence. | Process map and message templates |
| 7 | Legal and tax basics | What to ask the lawyer and when. Typical issues to raise early. Always flag that lawyer and accountant decide. | Lawyer briefing checklist |
| 8 | Case study and lessons learned | An anonymised off-plan luxury resort project: what worked, what failed, what I would change. | Debrief template |

Optional bonus: personal-feeling follow-up sequences instead of automation spam (4 short lessons, reuses your CRM thinking).

Rule for legal, tax and financing lessons: teach the issues and the questions, not the answers. Tell students to check with their own lawyer, accountant and bank. I have not verified Portuguese legal details in this pass, so any specific rule in these modules needs a lawyer's review before recording.

## Intro video script (target 75 seconds, about 190 words)

Visual plan: Mira on camera for the first 20 seconds, then cut to a 3D render turning into a finished building, then back to Mira.

> **[0:00]** Hello, I am Mira. I sell luxury property that does not exist yet.
>
> **[0:07]** For more than ten years I have worked in luxury off-plan and rehabilitation sales, and I run a 3D visualisation studio. I know what a buyer sees on day one: a plan, a render and a promise.
>
> **[0:20]** I came into real estate through [TBC: your real story in one sentence]. I have worked on a Lisbon residential rehabilitation project and an off-plan luxury resort project. What I love most is [TBC: your honest answer], the moment a buyer can finally picture their life there.
>
> **[0:45]** This course shows you how to sell that moment to international buyers, long before the building is finished. Branded residences, honest visuals, clear financing questions, and a calm sales process from first enquiry to contract.
>
> **[1:00]** Your first win comes in lesson one: you will map exactly what your buyers fear when nothing is built, and you will have a plan to answer it.
>
> **[1:08]** One question before you go. What is the one moment in your last sale where the buyer hesitated? Write it down. We will come back to it.

Notes:
- The credibility line "more than ten years" assumes the studio start in 2014. Confirm.
- No project, developer or employer names. Keep it that way in captions, thumbnails and metadata.

## Platform recommendation

**Recommended for launch: Thinkific or Teachable on a plan with no platform transaction fee, with a simple page and a paid pre-sale link.** For the validation phase, do not pay for a full platform at all: use a one-page site, an email tool and a payment link. Pick the course platform only after the go decision.

| Platform | What I found | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Teachable | Starter about $39/month with a 7.5% transaction fee. 0% fee from the $89/month Builder plan (2026 comparison). Free plan removed in 2026. One source adds a 3.9% + 30c fee on international cards. | Simple. Fees bite on the cheap tier. |
| Thinkific | Basic about $49/month, rising to $54 on 13 Aug 2026 per one source. Most sources say 0% platform fee, one source says 5% to 0.5% by tier. | Flexible. **Fee claims conflict, check the official page.** |
| Maven | One source: free until the first paid enrolment, then 10% plus payment fees. | Built for live cohorts. A poor fit for self-paced. Good later for a premium cohort. |
| Teachery | Listed in comparisons, not verified here. | Very simple. Check current price. |

Sources: [SchoolMaker comparison](https://schoolmaker.com/blog/thinkific-vs-teachable), [CartMango](https://cartmango.com/teachable-vs-thinkific/), [Teachery comparison](https://www.teachery.co/blog/online-course-platform-pricing-comparison-2026). These are affiliate-style comparison blogs. **Confirm every fee on the vendor's own pricing page before deciding.**

Also check before choosing: who handles EU VAT on digital services (merchant of record or you), payout currency, and whether your Swedish company or you personally are the seller. That is a tax question for your accountant. Keep this separate from your employer and from existing business accounts.
